## Full description

Plan-B brings your day together in one calm place: Today, a calendar, tasks, projects, notes,
habits, goals and focus sessions. It is designed for Persian and right-to-left first, with the
Jalali calendar and Persian digits, and switches to English and the Gregorian calendar whenever
you like.

**Today at a glance** — daily progress, a timeline of events and timed tasks, today's and
upcoming tasks, habits, focus, projects and recent notes. Reorder or hide any section.

**Tasks and projects** — subtasks, priorities, tags, projects, dates and times, reminders and
repeats (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly — in Jalali or Gregorian months). Inbox, Today,
Upcoming, Scheduled and Completed views, multi-select, swipe to complete or delete with undo.
Projects with milestones, notes and a board.

**Jalali and Gregorian calendar** — Day, Week, Month and Agenda views; all-day and timed events
with reminders and repeats; choose the first day of the week.

**Notebooks and notes** — notebooks, sections and notes with a block editor (text, headings,
checklists, lists, quotes, code), autosave, draft recovery, pin and favorite, tags and Markdown
import/export. Mixed Persian and English text displays correctly.

**Habits, goals and focus** — habits with counts and units, streaks and a heatmap; numeric goals
with pace and deadlines; Pomodoro-style focus sessions that stay accurate even if the app is
closed.

**Search, templates and weekly review** — fast search across everything (Arabic or Persian
keyboard letters), eight ready-made templates such as a daily planner and meeting notes, and a
weekly review of tasks, habits and focus.

**Real privacy** — everything stays on your phone. Plan-B has no Internet permission, no
account, no ads and no tracking. Backups and exports (CSV, JSON, Markdown) go only to files you
choose.

**Accessible and polished** — light and dark themes, a themed icon, large-font and TalkBack
support.
