# Plan-B 1.1.0

Introducing **Plan-B Pro**: 40 new features with a monthly subscription (399,000 toman) or a
lifetime purchase (1,999,000 toman) through Cafe Bazaar. Every feature of 1.0 stays free.

## A new first run

Plan-B now starts by asking for the language (Persian or English), then shows a welcome and
three animated slides.

## Pro features

- **Planning**: Persian natural-language quick add ("meeting with Ali tomorrow at 5pm"), advanced
  repeats, smart lists, deadlines, up to five reminders plus nagging until done, the Eisenhower
  matrix, task dependencies and a Gantt timeline.
- **Calendar and smart day**: official holidays and Hijri dates, device calendar sync,
  auto-planned days, drag-and-drop time blocking, a vertical timeline, morning and evening
  rituals.
- **Notes**: photos, files, tables, pen drawings, scans with text recognition, handwriting to
  text, voice recordings with transcripts, simple databases, formulas and charts, note links and
  version history, a note graph, a web clipper, writing mode, a daily journal and mood calendar.
- **Habits and focus**: Focus Pro with ambient sounds and strict mode (Do Not Disturb), Health
  Connect (steps, sleep, water, exercise, distance), advanced habit statistics with a year
  heatmap, challenges and badges, a mood and energy tracker.
- **Reports and personalization**: weekly, monthly and yearly statistics, a year report and PDF
  export, widgets, themes and icons, a quick-settings tile and shortcuts, a Wear OS app.
- **Security and data**: app lock and locked notes, automatic backups (keeps the last 21), a
  30-day trash and activity history.
- **AI and voice**: an assistant with your own key (Iranian gateways such as AvalAI listed first)
  to ask about your plan, plan your day or week, summarize or rewrite notes and break tasks into
  subtasks; Persian voice input. Every change is a preview first and can be undone.

## Permissions

Each permission is asked only when you use its feature, after an explanation: calendar (sync),
microphone (voice recordings and dictation), Do Not Disturb (Focus strict mode), Health Connect
(read only, just the kind you link to a habit) and Internet (only for the AI assistant).

## Privacy

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your data stays on your device; the AI assistant sends
only the text you choose, directly to the provider you choose.
