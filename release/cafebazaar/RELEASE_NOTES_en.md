# Plan-B 1.0.0

First public release of **Plan-B**, a calm, local-first planner and notebook in Persian and
English.

## Highlights

- **Today** dashboard with progress, timeline, tasks, habits, focus, projects and notes.
- **Tasks** with subtasks, priorities, tags, projects, reminders and recurring rules in Jalali
  or Gregorian months; Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Scheduled, Completed and Archived views.
- **Calendar** with Day, Week, Month and Agenda views.
- **Notebooks** with a block editor, autosave, draft recovery and Markdown import/export.
- **Habits**, **goals**, **focus sessions**, **templates**, **search** and a **weekly review**.
- **Backup & restore** (validated, transactional) and CSV/JSON/Markdown export.
- Persian (default) and English, right-to-left layout, Jalali/Gregorian calendars, light and
  dark themes, large-font and screen-reader friendly.

## Privacy

No Internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your data stays on your device.
